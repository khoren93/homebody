<h1 align="center">Homebody</h1>

<p align="center">
  A home workout planner and load calculator that runs entirely in your browser.<br>
  No account, no backend, no tracking.
</p>

Homebody builds a training plan from the equipment you actually own, works out
how much weight to lift in every set, and adjusts the numbers as you log your
sessions. Your data never leaves the device.

## Status

The project is under active development and is not usable yet. Progress is
tracked step by step in [docs/PROGRESS.md](docs/PROGRESS.md).

## Planned features

- **Planning** — full-body, upper/lower and push/pull/legs splits fitted to your
  schedule, session length and equipment.
- **Load calculator** — starting weights from body weight and experience, then
  progression driven by estimated one-rep max and reps in reserve.
- **Workout mode** — set tracker, rest timer, exercise photos and instructions.
- **Muscle map** — weekly volume per muscle, planned and performed.
- **Body log** — weight, girths, estimated body fat and progress photos.
- **Private by design** — everything is stored locally and works offline.
- **Localized** — English and Russian.
